# Suro

Suro is an adaptive AI tutor. A learner uploads a material, and Suro teaches it until the learner reaches mastery.

## Language

**Learner**:
An adult who uses Suro to understand a material.
_Avoid_: Student, user

**Material**:
A file that a learner uploads for Suro to teach.
_Avoid_: Document, lesson, upload, source

**Mastery**:
The state in which a learner has shown, by explaining or applying a concept, that they understand it.
_Avoid_: Demonstrated understanding, completion, proficiency

**Gap**:
A concept in a material for which the learner has not yet reached mastery.
_Avoid_: Weakness, mistake
